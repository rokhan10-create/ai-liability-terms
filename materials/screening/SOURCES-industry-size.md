# Sources for the industry-size ranking (2026-10-05)

- **Revenue:** U.S. Census Bureau, 2022 Economic Census, EC2200BASIC, U.S. level, all types of operation; taxable establishments where Census splits by tax status. File: https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector00/EC2200BASIC.zip (SHA-256 f310d333519284300501dad3e558dc96e27e0028733cd90d465c7949e48d01b2). Rail (4821) is outside Economic Census scope.
- **Household share, primary:** Census 2022 Economic Census, revenue by class of customer, EC2200CLCUST (household consumers and individuals / residential / individuals). File: https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector00/EC2200CLCUST.zip (SHA-256 0ab4444241d2623f784aaae0ea534eab20e675876b378b319bf236760558b801). Covers sectors 22, 44-45, 51 (telecom), 53 (rental), 54, 56, 72, 81 (repair).
- **Household share, fallback:** BEA 2017 benchmark Use Table, before redefinitions, producers' value, detail level: personal consumption expenditures (F01000) / total commodity output, mapped to NAICS via BEA's own concordance sheet. File: https://apps.bea.gov/industry/iTables%20Static%20Files/AllTablesIO.zip (SHA-256 ab316fa88157f8a96270334b1a6afa664fcf39a6c94f9c8e4ad061d930e81918).
- **Known limits:** PCE excludes household payments that BEA books elsewhere — real-estate broker commissions (residential investment), interest on consumer loans (personal interest payments), insurance-agency commissions (intermediate input to carriers) — so 5312, 5222/5223, and 5242 are understated. Cruise lines share a BEA code with freight shipping; sightseeing with transport support; apartment lessors with commercial landlords.
