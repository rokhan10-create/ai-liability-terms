# Who Answers for the Bot?

Project page for a working paper and dataset on how consumer-facing companies allocate liability for their own AI features (chatbots, shopping and travel agents, voice ordering).

**Live page:** https://rokhan10-create.github.io/ai-liability-terms/

Rohail Khan, Bellevue High School. Findings on the page are preliminary.

## Data and materials

The materials supporting the paper are in [`materials/`](materials/) (released October 2026; data frozen 14 October 2026). They contain:

- **Dataset.** One record per document, with binding status, assent mechanism, where the AI language sits, and the code for each of the eleven clause families.
- **Document register.** For every document: URL, retrieval date, stated effective date, capture method, SHA-256 hash, the coding-relevant extract and the coder's note.
- **November 2022 baselines.** The archived snapshot used for each pre-ChatGPT comparison, why any document has none, and the sentence-level comparison output.
- **Screening log.** Every firm screened, with its industry rank, revenue source, decision, reason and evidence link.
- **Coding manual and codebook.**
- **Analysis scripts.** Sample construction, the archival comparison, the firm-level taxonomy and the figures.
- **Reliability data.** Both coders' codes for the reliability-test documents.

The company documents themselves are copyrighted by the companies that publish them, so the repository gives each document's URL, archived-snapshot link and hash rather than its full text. Full captured texts are available to researchers on request.
